FAQ

What MSP owners ask before we start.

Are you a CPA firm?

No. Serenium provides accounting operations and controller services: the monthly close, PSA reconciliation and financial reporting. We don't perform audits or prepare income tax returns. We work alongside your CPA or tax preparer and hand them clean, reconciled books.

Which PSAs and accounting systems do you work with?

ConnectWise PSA first, plus Autotask and HaloPSA, with QuickBooks Online or Xero on the accounting side. We're not affiliated with any of these vendors; we just use them every day.

What access do you need? Can you move money?

For the Drift Report, read-only access is enough. For monthly work we use accountant-level users with two-factor authentication, never your password. We can prepare payments; you approve and release them.

Where are you based?

Serenium Management Group LLC is registered in Wyoming. All work is delivered remotely, on U.S. Eastern hours. We don't have a U.S. office and we won't pretend to.

What if I already have a bookkeeper?

That's common. The Drift Report shows what's being missed; from there we can work alongside your bookkeeper as the controller layer, or take over the close.
