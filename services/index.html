Services & pricing

Fixed prices. Scope in writing. No hourly surprises.

Every engagement starts the same way: a fixed-price Assessment that shows you where your books stand. From there, you decide whether to continue.

Financial Operations Assessment

$1,500–$2,500one time · fixed price, quoted before we start

A 10-day diagnostic of the real state of your books: reconciliations, the close process, and how your operating systems feed the ledger. You get a written report of what's wrong, quantified, with the root cause of each finding.

No obligation to continue. Sold on judgment, not software.

What's included

  • Every balance sheet account and its reconciliation, checked one by one

  • Integration check between your operating systems and the ledger
  • Findings quantified in dollars, with root causes

How pricing works

A few rules, stated up front.

No hourly billing

Monthly packages or fixed project prices. You know the number before we start.

Nothing below $2,000 a month

We take on 8 to 10 accounts in total. Every spot goes to a client who needs controller-level work.

6-month minimum

A clean close takes a few cycles to settle. Six months is enough to prove it works.

We don't prepare income tax returns or perform audits. We work alongside your CPA or tax preparer and hand them clean, reconciled books.
